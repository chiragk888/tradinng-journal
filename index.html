Intraday Momentum Buying

Fast expansion trades targeting liquidity breaks or trend continuation above/below VWAP.

  • Strike: ATM or 1-Strike ITM (Delta ~0.55)
  • SL: 18-22 Nifty Spot Points (~12% premium)
  • Target: 35-50 Spot Points (1:2+ Risk-Reward)
  • Time Window: 09:20-10:30 AM or 01:15-03:00 PM
Vertical Spread VIX: 11.0–15.0

Bull Call / Bear Put Spread

Defined-risk directional setup that lowers capital requirements and hedges against IV crush.

  • Buy ATM + Sell OTM (+150/200 Pts)
  • Max Debit: ≤ 35% of Spread Width
  • SL: 50% Net Debit Paid
  • Exit: 75%-80% of Max Theoretical Profit
0-DTE Expiry VIX: 13.5–17.0

Thursday Short Straddle

Non-directional income capture exploiting rapid Thursday theta decay in range-bound markets.

  • Sell ATM CE + Sell ATM PE (09:20 AM Entry)
  • SL: +25% Hard Stop on Individual Leg
  • Combined SL: 20% Portfolio Cap
  • Profit Target: 50% Decay of Combined Credit
Defined-Risk Neutral VIX: > 14.0

Iron Fly (Wings Protection)

Combines short straddle credit with outer long protective options to cap tail risk.

  • Sell ATM Straddle + Buy Wings (±250 Pts)
  • Breakeven: ATM Strike ± Net Credit
  • Target: 35%-50% Net Credit Captured
  • Invalidation: 15-min close beyond Breakeven
Price Action Reversal Any VIX

Liquidity Sweep & FVG

High-probability intraday trade catching institutional sweeps above PDH/PDL into imbalances.

  • Sweep PDH/PDL + Rejection Candle
  • Entry: Limit order at 50% FVG Retracement
  • SL: 2 Pts beyond extreme sweep wick
  • Target: Opposite Liquidity Pool (1:2.5+ RR)
